# The Mission

This file is required reading alongside `CLAUDE.md` and `INDEX.md` — it's
the "who/what/why" behind the Private/Personal projects, not just a list of
what's being built. Read it before offering opinions on sequencing,
priority, or what matters right now.

## What this actually is

The user is building his life, deliberately, across four dimensions:
**spiritual, financial, physical, and mental** — and recording the process
so that those who come after him can learn from it. He has kept personal
diaries for years already. He knows the honest statistics on how many
people read or watch something daily — very few, unless you become
genuinely famous — and is proceeding anyway. The point is not audience
size. The point is leaving an honest, complete record, and making a clear
path for the few who do want to follow it.

**The deep source of truth for what he's building is
`git@github.com:mrsuber/family-social-site-pes-01.git`**
(indexed here as [Family Social Site](projects/family-social-site/PROJECT.md),
cloned at `~/dev/myWebProjects/family-social-site-pes-01`) — despite the
name, that repo is his personal life-operating-system: Commander Diary,
Commander Calendar, biography tracking, financial dashboard, global
assets/landmarks, and an Islamic course LMS. Check that repo for detail this
file doesn't cover. This file is the condensed version for quick context at
the start of a session, not a replacement for it.

## The current sequence

This is the actual order, not a menu to reprioritize without being told:

1. **Study and practice Islam** — not just nightly Quran memorization
   (roughly 4am until Subhi/Fajr prayer), but Islam comprehensively:
   theology, fiqh, governance and business-ethics principles. This is not
   separate from the builds below — Islam is the deliberately-chosen
   *governing framework* for the whole SuberCraftex/SuberFood ecosystem
   (see `MASTER-MISSION-DOCUMENT.md`'s "Islamic Governance Framework" in
   the family-social-site repo — no riba/interest, zakat, halal standards,
   Shura consultation, fair treatment, all specified as how the actual
   business is meant to run, "NOT a religious decoration or marketing" in
   the document's own words). Ongoing, running in parallel with everything
   below, not something "finished" before the rest starts. Tracked at
   [Islamic Study & Practice](projects/islamic-study/PROJECT.md).
2. **Build SuberCraftex** — including actually completing the curriculum
   embedded in that repo (`CURRICULUM_MASTER_PLAN.md` — a genuinely
   enormous multi-year skills path, woodworking through metalworking,
   machining, composites, up to aerospace-level fabrication) — governed
   throughout by the Islamic framework above, not just built as ordinary
   software/business.
3. **Build SuberFood** next, after SuberCraftex.
4. **Publish all of it on YouTube** — the struggles, the frustrations, the
   successes, honestly, not just the highlight reel — so there's a clear
   path for anyone who wants to follow the same road. This is what the
   DaVinci Resolve MCP setup, the AI Voiceover clone, and the Figma plugin
   (for covers/thumbnails) all exist for.

## What "help through this journey" means

new idea silently reorder it), and helping turn the day-to-day
struggle/frustration/success into something worth publishing — without
sanding off the honesty that's the actual point of documenting this.
